# 0026 — The engagement red line is a mandatory decision gate for every surface

Date: 2026-07-07

## Status

Superseded by [0035](0035-engagement-mechanics-chosen-measured-switchable.md) on 2026-10-07. Previously Accepted (2026-07-20 — first gate exercise passed on the promotion-card fixture; red-line table in fixtures/content-hoarder/README.md).

## Context

Pass 08 (the 2026-07-07 dark-patterns research pass) synthesized three converging
frameworks (Center for Humane Technology, Harris, Self-Determination Theory) into a
testable red line. Pass 08 Do-first #1: make it a decision gate, not a principle.

## Decision

- An engagement mechanic is manipulation when it: (1) exploits a cognitive bias to
  override reflective preference, OR (2) conceals its mechanism, OR (3) serves system
  goals (engagement/retention) at the user's expense, OR (4) uses punishment (shame,
  guilt, social comparison, loss) as the lever.
- The test, applied to every new surface/feature before it ships: **"Would the user
  endorse this mechanism if they understood it fully and in advance?"** No → manipulation,
  rejected. ADHD addendum: does it exploit dopamine dysregulation, time blindness, RSD, or
  executive dysfunction? Yes → inaccessible at minimum, rejected.
- The ethical-repurposing catalog (Pass 08 §2) is the approved substitution table:
  variable reward → reason-tagged novelty surfacing (known time, one item, reason shown);
  loss aversion → neutral-frame awareness ("last visited Tuesday"); streaks → bounded
  sprint framing (resets without loss); leaderboards → optional private witness (share a
  win manually, no ranking); infinite scroll → finite batches with explicit done states;
  escalation → adaptive capacity ("5 minutes today?" — responds to today, never compares
  to yesterday); guilt re-engagement → curiosity re-entry (0023).
- Genuine deadlines (a real bill date, a real meeting) are informational, not manufactured
  urgency — real urgency has honest provenance; manufactured urgency has none.
- Progress is narrative, not scoreboard: "You've been building your machine learning
  foundation," with numbers as supporting evidence only, never the headline
  (Bandura/McAdams, Verified).

## Alternatives considered

- Keep the red line as a documented principle only — rejected per Pass 08 Do-first #1
  (principles drift; gates get checked).
- A formal external ethics review — overkill at personal scale.

## Consequences

- Every future surface ADR/fixture includes a one-line red-line check in its done-when.
- Open question carried honestly: whether reason-tagged surfacing fully satisfies the
  novelty need without a compulsive loop is a Hypothesis (Pass 08 Open Q1) — the design
  stands regardless.

## Reactivation condition

None — this is the constitution-level constraint. Sharpen the checklist wording as gates
get exercised.
