# khaos roadmap

This roadmap describes product directions rather than a deployment schedule. Individual components may be at different stages; see their repositories for current implementation status.

## Capture and knowledge

- Keep capture quick across desktop, mobile, and integrations while preserving source context.
- Make the handoff from saved content to durable knowledge explicit and reviewable.
- Improve retrieval and resurfacing without creating an obligation to process every item.

## Planning and re-entry

- Surface a small, explainable set of options and one practical next action.
- Support returning after a lapse without guilt, loss framing, or mandatory catch-up.
- Make engagement features optional and responsive to user feedback.

## Safety and interoperability

- Preserve provenance through capture, promotion, and action receipts.
- Gate external or destructive actions on scoped capabilities and user approval.
- Keep canonical data portable and distinguish it from derived indexes and views.
- Explore integrations and deployment options without assuming a single device or provider.

See the [architecture map](architecture.md), [contracts](contracts.md), and [onramps](onramps.md) for the underlying design.
